Meet the founders

Two engineers from western Kentucky — one mechanical, one controls — who started Bar H to build docks the way they would want one built.

Co-founder · Mechanical engineer

Tristan Hagan

I was born and raised in Henderson, KY. My years were spent enjoying hobbies that included sports, the outdoors, and working on our family farm. As an adult, I pursued a degree in Mechanical Engineering, which was driven by my passion for solving the toughest problems.

My career as an engineer began in the lab: analyzing, predicting, and evaluating results from the failures and successes we experienced. Following this, I spent time in an automotive quality engineer role, where I learned the value of effective, robust processes and accuracy. That experience allowed me to transition into a design/project engineering role.

I am now looking to utilize this skillset to provide superb service through Bar H Solutions to individuals and businesses that desire perfection.

Hometown
Henderson, KY
Background
Lab analysis, automotive quality engineering, design/project engineering
Off the clock
Sports, the outdoors, the family farm

Co-founder · Controls engineer

Jake Bartlett

Over the course of my life I have always been a hands-on learner. I find a great deal of satisfaction in being able to build and repair things on my own.

I have spent the last five years of my career working as a controls engineer for a respectable company in Madisonville, KY. I specialize in designing, building, and troubleshooting industrial equipment and assembly lines. In my free time I enjoy hunting, carpentry, and most of all spending time on the water with good friends and family.

I have always dreamed of being a small business owner, as it runs in my family. My vision for Bar H Solutions is to provide satisfaction to each and every person we serve.

Career
Five years as a controls engineer in Madisonville, KY
Specialty
Designing, building and troubleshooting industrial equipment and assembly lines
Off the clock
Hunting, carpentry, time on the water
