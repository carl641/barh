River or lake: two different docks

Owensboro owners usually have one of two kinds of water in mind. The river and the Corps lakes to the east ask very different things of a dock.

The Newburgh pool

Newburgh Locks and Dam, downstream, holds Owensboro’s normal low level. Above that the river rises with every wet spring and with heavy rain upstream, so the dock is anchored for the high water, not the pool.

A working river

Towboats push past downtown around the clock. Their wakes, and the drift that comes down in high water, decide where along your bank the dock can sit safely.

Rough River is different

The Corps runs Rough River Lake for flood control, and it’s drawn down a long way for winter. Private docks there are allowed only where the lake’s shoreline plan zones for them — check before you buy a lot for the dock.

Planning a dock in Owensboro

The paperwork and the engineering both start from the same few facts about the lot. Here’s what we’ll ask.

Start with a quote
  • Corps approvalA dock in the Ohio needs Section 10 approval from the Corps’ Louisville District. On a Corps lake it needs a shoreline use permit instead.
  • Kentucky floodplainWork in the Ohio’s floodplain can need a permit from the Kentucky Division of Water as well.
  • Where your line endsWhether your deed runs to the water’s edge, or stops at a levee, road or railroad, changes who has to agree to the dock.
  • Winter depthOn a lake, the depth that matters is winter pool, not summer. A slip that floats in July can sit on mud in February.

Owensboro dock questions

Do you build docks in Owensboro?

Yes. Owensboro and Daviess County are part of the Ohio River Valley we serve from our shop in Providence.

Is a dock on the river or on a lake the better buy?

It depends how you’ll use it. A river dock is right on your doorstep but has to be engineered for current, wakes and big seasonal swings. A lake dock sees calmer water but is bound by the lake’s shoreline rules and its winter drawdown. We’ll talk through both before you commit.

How long does the walkway to a river dock need to be?

Long enough to stay walkable from low pool to high water at your bank. On a tall, steep bank that can be a lot of gangway; on a gentle slope much less. Photos of the bank at low water tell us most of what we need.

Can I put a dock on Rough River Lake?

Only where the Corps’ shoreline plan allows private docks, so ask the lake office about your lot first. If it’s allowed, the dock has to be designed for the lake’s deep winter drawdown.
