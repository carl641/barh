The Cumberland

The Cumberland River runs the length of the corridor — through Nashville, past Clarksville and Dover, and on into Lake Barkley.

Lakes near Nashville

The big lakes around Nashville are Corps of Engineers lakes, and each one has its own rules on where private docks can go.
