The Tennessee side of Kentucky Lake

Kentucky Lake crosses the state line without changing its rules, but the water off Henry County comes in two very different kinds.

Same lake, same TVA

Crossing into Tennessee doesn’t change who approves a dock: it’s TVA’s Section 26a on this side of the line too.

The Big Sandy’s long arms

The Big Sandy and West Sandy reach miles inland. They’re sheltered and good for a dock, but their upper ends go shallow when the lake drops five feet for winter.

Wide water at Paris Landing

Around the US 79 bridge the main lake is wide and busy, with the barge channel out in the middle. Main-lake docks here are built for wakes and wind.

Paris dock questions

Do you build docks in Tennessee around Paris?

Yes. Henry County’s side of Kentucky Lake — Paris Landing, Buchanan and the Big Sandy — is part of the area we serve.

Who approves a dock on the Tennessee side of Kentucky Lake?

TVA, under Section 26a — the same as on the Kentucky side.

Our lake house is a weekend place. What dock needs the least looking after?

Aluminum. The frame won’t rot or rust, and with composite or aluminum decking there’s nothing to stain or replace between visits.

Is the Big Sandy too shallow for a dock?

Not at all near the main lake, but the upper ends get shallow at winter pool. Tell us where the lot is and we’ll look at the depth there.
