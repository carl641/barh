Kentucky Lake from the Calloway shore

Kentucky Lake is wide off Calloway County, and the difference between a main-lake lot and a cove lot shows up in every part of the dock.

Wind and fetch

On the main lake the wind has miles of open water to build waves. A main-lake dock is oriented and built for it; a cove dock gets an easier life.

359 down to 354

TVA fills the lake in spring and draws it down about five feet for winter. Coves can go shallow, so the dock is sited for winter depth.

What’s between you and the water

Whether the strip along the shore is your land, land with a TVA flowage easement or TVA-owned land changes what can be built and who approves it.

Murray dock questions

Do you build docks around Murray?

Yes. Calloway County’s side of Kentucky Lake is part of the Kentucky Lake & Lake Barkley area we serve.

Does TVA have to approve a dock on Kentucky Lake?

Yes. Kentucky Lake is a TVA reservoir, and a new dock needs approval under Section 26a of the TVA Act before it’s built.

Is a main-lake lot or a cove lot better for a dock?

A cove is gentler on the dock and the boat, but can go shallow in winter. The main lake has the depth and the view, and the wind and waves that come with them. Either works if the dock is built for it.

How big can a private dock on Kentucky Lake be?

TVA caps the footprint of private docks, so the size question is answered inside its limits. We design the slips, roof and deck to make the most of it.
