Two lakes, two rulebooks

Kentucky Lake and Lake Barkley share a canal and a water level, but they answer to different agencies — and which lake your lot is on decides who signs off on the dock.

Kentucky Lake: TVA

Kentucky Lake is a Tennessee Valley Authority reservoir. A new dock there needs TVA approval under Section 26a before it's built.

Lake Barkley: the Corps

Lake Barkley is run by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers. Private docks need a Corps shoreline permit, and not every stretch of shoreline allows one.

One water level

Both lakes sit near 359 feet in summer and drop about five feet for winter, so a dock has to work at both levels.

Questions we get

Do you build on both Kentucky Lake and Lake Barkley?

Yes — both lakes, in Kentucky and across the line into Tennessee around Paris and Buchanan.

Who approves a new dock on Kentucky Lake or Lake Barkley?

On Kentucky Lake, the Tennessee Valley Authority, under Section 26a. On Lake Barkley, the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, through a shoreline permit that's only available where the shoreline allows private docks.

Do you build in lake towns that aren't listed here?
