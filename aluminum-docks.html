Standard features

Every aluminum dock comes with the same three things: a frame of custom-extruded “C” profile drawn for dock work, that same extrusion carried through the dock behind a Limited Lifetime Warranty, and decking that cannot rot.

Each aluminum dock frame is constructed using a custom-extruded “C” profile engineered specifically for dock applications. This design delivers a clean, visually appealing exterior while providing exceptional structural strength.

A similar “C” style extrusion is used in many locations around the dock. Whether you choose a hip roof or a gable roof — an aluminum dock from Bar H gives you the confidence that it is built to last. Even more — your dock will come with a Limited Lifetime Warranty.

On any aluminum dock, you can choose between composite decking with fiberglass reinforcement or LockDry aluminum decking. Both options deliver a seamless, rot-free solution built for long-term performance.

Aluminum or steel?

Aluminum gives you a frame that will not rust, a lighter dock on the water and an extruded finish that steel cannot match up close. If first cost matters most, our steel dock is the budget option — a covered gable-roof dock on fabricated steel trusses.

Questions we get

Is an aluminum dock strong enough?

Yes — strength comes from the section, not the metal alone. The extrusions are engineered for the loads a covered dock actually sees, and the same dock has to stand up on open water everywhere else these are built.

How does it compare on price?

It is usually the higher first cost against a comparable steel build, and the lower one to own over twenty years because there is no rust to chase. We will quote both if you want to see the difference on your layout.

What is Flotation Systems?

An aluminum boat dock manufacturer that has been building since 1992. Bar H Solutions is a proud dealer — we handle the engineering for your site, the order, the delivery and the install.

Do you build docks for HOAs and municipalities?

Yes. Commercial and community docks — for neighborhoods, parks, marinas and resorts — are one of our aluminum dock styles. Tell us about the project and how many slips and people it needs to serve.

Do you build on Kentucky Lake and Lake Barkley?

Yes — both shores, plus the Cumberland River arm, the Ohio River around Henderson, Owensboro and Evansville, and the Clarksville–Nashville corridor.
