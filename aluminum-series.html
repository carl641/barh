What sets the Aluminum Series apart

Three things decide how a covered dock lives on this water: the roof, the frame under it, and whether either was drawn for your shoreline.

That is the whole argument, and on water it is a strong one. No sacrificial coating to keep up with, no rust bleed down a post, and a frame that is light enough to float on less flotation than the steel equivalent.

The parts are custom-engineered extrusions rather than stock shapes adapted to fit. That is why the fit and finish reads the way it does up close — the joints were drawn to meet each other.

Bar H Solutions is a proud dealer of Flotation Systems, Inc., which has been producing top-quality aluminum boat docks since 1992. We engineer your layout, they build the aluminum, and we set it on your shoreline.

Aluminum or steel?

Steel gives you mass, and the Premier and Timber Series use it well. Aluminum gives you a frame that will not rust, a lighter dock on the water and an extruded finish that steel cannot match up close. If the deciding factor is long-term upkeep rather than first cost, this is the series to look at.

Questions we get

Is an aluminum dock strong enough?

Yes — strength comes from the section, not the metal alone. The extrusions are engineered for the loads a covered dock actually sees, and the same dock has to stand up on open water everywhere else these are built.

How does it compare on price?

It is usually the higher first cost against a comparable steel build, and the lower one to own over twenty years because there is no rust to chase. We will quote both if you want to see the difference on your layout.

What is Flotation Systems?

An aluminum boat dock manufacturer that has been building since 1992. Bar H Solutions is a proud dealer — we handle the engineering for your site, the order, the delivery and the install.

Do you build on Kentucky Lake and Lake Barkley?

Yes — both shores, plus the Cumberland River arm, the Ohio River around Henderson, Owensboro and Evansville, and the Clarksville–Nashville corridor.
