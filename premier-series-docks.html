Standard features

These three come standard on every Premier build.

Hip Roof

Our Premier Series hip roof design and composition is second to none in the world of boat docks. Each dock is handcrafted in our shop before it goes to the galvanizer, ensuring the ultimate fit and finish. We take great pride in our roof structures and can confidently say they are stronger than any other option available on the market, providing exceptional value in dock pricing.

Decorative Walkway

We offer truss and tube frame walkways for boat docks. Each option can be arched or flat, and our walkways include sturdy, decorative handrails. This combination of components allows us to build the strongest and most visually appealing walkways on the water, ensuring you get the best value for your dock pricing.

Rot-Free Construction

Whether you desire composite or concrete for your boat docks, our Premier Series Dock is constructed without the use of lumber. Unlike other builders who commonly use lumber for stringers under decking, fascia boards, and bumper mounts, choosing the Premier Series means you get a superior product without these materials.

Why the hip roof costs more

Four slopes mean four hip rafters, a shorter ridge and more cuts than a gable of the same footprint — more fabrication for the same square feet of cover. What you get back is a roof with no flat end wall, and a dock that looks finished from every direction on the water rather than only from the front.

Questions we get

What is the difference between the Premier and the Timber Series?

Both are steel. The Premier carries a hip roof, which slopes on all four sides. The Timber carries a gable roof built on fully fabricated steel tube trusses. The frame engineering follows the same approach on both — the roofline is the decision you are actually making.

How do you size the slip?

From the boats. Tell us length, beam and how you want to walk around them, and whether a lift is going in now or later. Slip width is drawn from that, not from a catalog size.

Do you build on Kentucky Lake and Lake Barkley?

Yes — both shores, plus the Cumberland River arm, the Ohio River around Henderson, Owensboro and Evansville, and the Clarksville–Nashville corridor.

Who engineers the dock?

Bar H Solutions was founded by two engineers, Tristan Hagan and Jake Bartlett. The design work on your dock is theirs, not a subcontractor's.
