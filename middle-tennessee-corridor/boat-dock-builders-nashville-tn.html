Three lakes, three shoreline plans

All three lakes are run by the Corps’ Nashville District, but they behave differently and each has its own rules on private docks.

Old Hickory

The most built-up of the three, with private docks along much of its residential shoreline. It’s held within a few feet through the year, which is kind to a dock — but it’s busy, so wakes count.

Percy Priest

Drawn down several feet each winter, with a lot of public shoreline. Check the zoning in front of the lot before planning a dock: on some stretches it rules a private dock out.

Cheatham & the Cumberland

Below Nashville the Cumberland is a run-of-river lake with current and barge traffic, closer to a river dock than a lake dock.

Planning a dock in Nashville

On a Corps lake the permit decides as much as the engineering does. Here’s what a Nashville dock project runs through.

Start with a quote
  • Shoreline zoningPrivate docks are only allowed along shoreline the lake’s plan sets aside for them. Ask the lake office about your lot before anything else.
  • Shoreline use permitA new dock needs a permit from the Corps’ Nashville District. Its rules cover dock size, location and materials — such as encapsulated flotation.
  • Buying a lake house?Corps dock permits don’t transfer with the house. A new owner applies in their own name, and the dock has to be in good repair to get it.
  • Keeping it permittedPermits are renewed periodically and docks are inspected. A dock that won’t rot or rust makes that a formality.

Nashville dock questions

Do you build boat docks in Nashville?

Yes. The Clarksville–Nashville corridor is home to a substantial share of our customers. Tell us which lake and where the lot is.

Which Nashville lake is easiest to build a dock on?

It’s less about the lake than about the shoreline in front of your lot. Each lake’s plan zones where private docks are allowed, so two lots on the same lake can get different answers.

Does the dock permit transfer when we buy a lake house?

Generally not on a Corps lake. The new owner applies for the permit in their own name, and the existing dock has to meet current rules and be in good repair.

We also have a place at Kentucky Lake. Can you build there?

Yes — many of the docks we build are on Kentucky Lake and Lake Barkley.
