Small lakes, still water

Hopkins County doesn’t have a big reservoir, but it has a lot of smaller lakes — and they’re kind to a dock.

Still water

No current, no barge wakes and little change in level through the year. Anchoring is simpler, and a fixed dock is often an option.

Whose lake is it?

City lakes, private lakes and association lakes each set their own rules on docks — and it’s usually the lake’s owner, not a federal agency, who decides.

Old mine lakes

Many lakes here sit on reclaimed coal ground. They’re often deep and steep-sided close to the bank, which shapes how the dock is anchored.

Madisonville dock questions

How close are you to Madisonville?

Our shop is in Providence, about 20 miles away. Madisonville and Hopkins County are the closest work we do.

Can I put a dock on Lake Peewee?

Lake Peewee belongs to the City of Madisonville, so the city decides what can be built on its shoreline. Check with them about your lot first.

Does a small lake still need an engineered dock?

Yes, though it’s a simpler job. Depth, bottom, bank and how you’ll use the dock still decide its size, its anchoring and whether it floats.

Can you come out and look at the spot?

Yes. Being this close, a site visit is easy to arrange — ask when you request a quote.
