How a dock gets built

Same sequence every time, whether it's a single-slip aluminum dock or a full Premier build.

Talk it through

Tell us the lot, the water, and the boats. We'll walk you through which series makes sense before anything is drawn.

Engineer the design

Your dock is engineered to your shoreline — slip sizes, water depth, fluctuation, and wind and wave exposure.

Fabricate in-house

Frames, trusses, and components are fabricated to the drawings so everything fits the first time on the water.

Deliver and install

We deliver, set, and anchor the dock on site, then hand it over ready for the boat.
