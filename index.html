How a dock gets built

Same sequence every time, whether it's a single-slip platform dock or a community dock for a whole neighborhood.

Talk it through

Tell us the lot, the water, and the boats. We'll walk you through which kind of dock makes sense before anything is drawn.

Engineer the design

Your dock is engineered to your shoreline — slip sizes, water depth, fluctuation, and wind and wave exposure.

Fabricate in-house

Frames, trusses, and components are fabricated to the drawings so everything fits the first time on the water.

Deliver and install

We deliver, set, and anchor the dock on site, then hand it over ready for the boat.

About Bar H Solutions

Bar H Solutions was founded to serve an industry that has long been underserved. Today Tristan Hagan leads the company, backed by a team that carries every dock from the first drawing to the day it’s on the water. Our goal is the highest-quality products, innovative solutions, and exceptional service for the customers we value.

Founder · Mechanical engineer

Tristan Hagan

I was born and raised in Henderson, KY. My years were spent enjoying hobbies that included sports, the outdoors, and working on our family farm. As an adult, I pursued a degree in Mechanical Engineering, which was driven by my passion for solving the toughest problems.

My career as an engineer began in the lab: analyzing, predicting, and evaluating results from the failures and successes we experienced. Following this, I spent time in an automotive quality engineer role, where I learned the value of effective, robust processes and accuracy. That experience allowed me to transition into a design/project engineering role.

I am now looking to utilize this skillset to provide superb service through Bar H Solutions to individuals and businesses that desire perfection.
